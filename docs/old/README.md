# Archived documents

These are the project's original write-ups, **superseded by `docs/NOTES.md`**,
which unions their substantive content into one organized account (with the
fabricated/uncommitted material corrected or removed). They are kept only for
their longer proofs, tables, and history; where any of them disagrees with
`docs/NOTES.md`, `NOTES.md` is current.

- `PAPER.md` — the previous consolidated narrative (the direct ancestor of `NOTES.md`).
- `HANDOFF.md` — the "continuation" note; §3–§4 (the still-unbuilt local-search
  planner spec and its measurement protocol) are the only parts not folded into
  `NOTES.md`.
- `sources/HEURISTIC-BOUNDS.md` — the admissible-heuristic / IDA* proofs.
- `sources/SORTING-BOUNDS.md` — the merge-family sorters and their bounds.
- `sources/operation-count-theory.md` — the fullest operation-count treatment + literature.
- `sources/cycle-model-theory.md` — the whole-cycle (permutation-distance) theory.
- `sources/original-notes.md` — the project's seed notes (superseded by `cycle-model-theory.md`).

Internal cross-references between these files were written before the move and
may point at the old (`docs/…`) paths; read them as relative to this `old/` tree.
